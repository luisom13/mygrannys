Our story, rooted in Charleston

Amanda grew up in Charleston, South Carolina, in the creek behind the house and out at the beach. She also grew up with allergies, and with ear infections every time water got trapped in her ears.

Unless she was with her granny. After a swim, Granny had drops of vinegar and herbs ready for her ears, and local honey for the allergies.

“It always amazed me at how well it always worked.”
Amanda Ogier, founder

She started My Granny's in 2024 to honor her grandmother and to share what she knows about the ways nature can care for our bodies. Every blend is still made by hand, in small batches.
